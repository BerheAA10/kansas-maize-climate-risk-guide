# Public data policy

This dashboard is intended as a **research showcase**, not as a mirror of the complete DSSAT archive.

## Public repository: acceptable
- regional and statewide derivative CSV tables
- model coefficient/penalty summaries
- publication-ready PNG maps/figures/heatmaps
- methods/reproducibility documentation
- scripts needed to recreate the public derivative bundle from validated local outputs

## Keep private / local
- complete 5,063,424 treatment-year masters
- DSSAT executable/model installation
- raw weather/input directories unless separately cleared for redistribution
- local H:\ or F:\ source projects
- temporary or failed SAFE runs
- credentials and Streamlit secrets

The export script copies files into a **new timestamped bundle** and does not modify validated source projects.
